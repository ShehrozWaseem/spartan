3
State sales-tax portals · built 26 Sep 2026

From 1,018 portal PDFs to one reviewable workbook

The WestMark team logged into each state's tax portal and pulled the relevant information for that state: payment pages, balance pages and filed returns. A script read every PDF, recognised which kind of page it was, lifted the figures, and wrote each one as a row that points back to its page. Nothing was netted, merged or interpreted. This page shows the route from PDF to row, and lets you open any document and see both sides.

4
Exposure with payments · built 28 Sep 2026

What each state says is owed, next to what was actually paid

This workbook puts two things side by side for every state: what the state's website showed as owed on 10 September 2026, and what the saved portal pages show was reported and paid. It exists so that anyone can ask "where did this number come from, and can I trust it?" and get an answer that ends at a page in a PDF. Nothing in it is typed by hand.

Project status · built 28 Sep 2026

Filed, paid and collected, state by state, on one sheet

The status workbook is the one-page answer for the project. For every state and year it shows what Spartan reported to the state, what the state received, what penalties were added, and what customers were actually charged. Four columns were filled in by script; every other column is the template's own sum. This page shows where each of those four comes from and how far you can trust it.
